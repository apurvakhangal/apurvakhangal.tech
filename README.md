## Design Philosophy <3

When building this portfolio, I focused on a few key things:

* **Simplicity over complexity** – The layout is minimal and readable. Your projects should be the star, not flashy effects.
* **Subtle animations** – Animations should enhance the experience, not distract from it. Every transition has a purpose.
* **Smooth interactions** – Things should feel responsive and predictable. Hover effects, scroll behaviors, and transitions are carefully timed.
* **Natural flow** – The page guides you through the content in a way that feels intuitive, from introduction to projects to contact.

The design philosophy is simple: **clarity first, creativity second**. Technology should get out of the way and let the work speak for itself.
